Dear Paruu, my favourite chocplate… 🍫💗

I don't think you fully realise how much happiness you have brought into my life. Before you, I had days when I felt stuck, lonely, and disappointed in myself. Then I got to know you—your stories, your random thoughts, your caring side, your silly side, and the way you somehow manage to make me smile even when my day has been boring.

I love your smile. Seriously, how can one smile be this unfair? And your eyes, your hair, your little expressions, the way you look when you're being all cute and dramatic—Paruu, please let me live. 😭💗 And that bindi? A whole separate chapter. One tiny bindi and my brain forgets how to function. 🫠

And beyond how beautiful you look, I love the person you are. I love how you listen, how you care, how you explain things when I'm confused, and how you support me when I don't feel confident in myself. Sometimes you become my maa, sometimes my best friend, sometimes the person I want to annoy for absolutely no reason—and somehow, you are still my favourite person through all of it.

Thank you for being there for me. Thank you for believing that I can do better. I'm going to keep trying—not because I need to prove everyone wrong, but because I want to become someone I'm proud of, too. And I hope I can give you the same kind of comfort, support, and belief that you give me.

Also, just so we're clear: the “favourite chocplate” thing is not going away. And one day, when you least expect it, I will remind you about that lip-gloss taste joke. 😭😂 No pressure, madam; your comfort and your boundaries always come first. For now, I'll settle for your smile and the privilege of being the person who gets to tease you.

Happy Birthday to my Raani, my Paruu, my chatpati naari. I hope this year is gentle with you, brings you confidence, peace, laughter, good health, and so many reasons to smile. You deserve to feel loved not just on your birthday, but on ordinary days too.

And one more thing: you believed in a version of me I was still trying to become. I won't forget that.

Tumhara Aniket ♡
